How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above and wait for the download to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file QuranDuas.apk (from the notification or your Files / Downloads app).
  3. If your phone says installs from this source aren't allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
Updating: apps installed this way don't update automatically. Come back to this page and install the newest version over the old one. Your settings and bookmarks are kept. Once the app is on Google Play, you can switch to it for automatic updates.

Is it safe?

Yes. This is the official app, signed by the developer, Syed Muhammad Wajih Ali. It has no ads, no account and no tracking. Read the Privacy Policy.
